Explore sarcoma evidence profiles

Move the sliders to project a hypothetical evidence profile into the fixed AWRS-SMC atlas. Hover over an original point for its study information and Kaplan–Meier evidence curve. Contours show clusters of existing observations. The star is a synthetic profile, not a study or a treatment recommendation. Slider values use the selected evidence features; names ending in log10p denote log10(1 + value). They are not patient-level measurements.

Full 600-observation evidence map

Each cluster in the legend is named after the disease and therapy that clearly dominate its members when one does (hover for more clinical context), or after the one or two features that most set its members apart from the rest of the atlas when no single disease/therapy dominates — either way, a description of the group as a whole, not any single observation. Click a cluster's name above to jump every slider to the values of the evidence profile closest to that cluster's center, as a representative starting point. A colored ring identifies the exact observed point. The red star is its approximate Transformer prediction and may fall in another cluster. Different time windows from one study can belong to different clusters.
